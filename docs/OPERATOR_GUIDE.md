# How to use Packs Be Slippin'

## First time only (about 5 minutes)

1. Double-click the zip file, then drag **Packs Be Slippin'** into your **Applications** folder.
2. Double-click **Packs Be Slippin'**. A message says Apple can't check it. Click **Done**. This is expected.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**. Scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to Packs Be Slippin'. Enter your Mac password, then click **Open Anyway** again. (On older Macs: hold **Control**, click the app, choose **Open**, then **Open**.)
4. Load your spreadsheet (see below). The first time, the app asks you to **match columns**. Check each row, then click **Save Matches**. You won't be asked again.

## Every week (about 2 minutes)

1. In Google Sheets, open the order sheet and choose **File → Download → Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)**. It lands in your **Downloads** folder.
2. Open **Packs Be Slippin'**.
3. **Drag the downloaded file** onto the big box (or click **Browse…** and pick it).
4. Check the **Delivery date**. It remembers your usual delivery day, so normally it's already right. Use the dropdown or **Calendar…** to change it. This date prints on every slip.
5. Optional, drivers: drag in your **driver list** (or click **Driver List…**) and check the columns. Each slip then shows its driver, and the PDF starts with each driver's delivery list.
6. Click **Generate Pack Slips for (date)**.
7. Click **Open PDF to Print**, then press **⌘P**. Pickup bag labels: **Generate Bag Labels**, print on the Zebra at **Actual size**.

The PDF is saved in the **same folder as your spreadsheet**, named **Pack Slips – (spreadsheet name) – delivery (date).pdf**.

## A second business

Each business has its own **layout** (columns, logo, delivery day). Drop in its spreadsheet and the app switches layout by itself. The first time, choose **make a new layout**, check the matches, then add the logo in **Edit Layout…**.

## Updates

When a new version is ready, the app asks when it opens. Click **Update Now**: it closes and reopens by itself in about a minute, and your layouts are kept. (You can also click **Check for Updates** at the bottom of the window.)

## If something looks wrong

- **New dishes this week?** Nothing to do. Every column between **Address** and **Delivery Fee** is treated as a menu item, so new or renamed dishes are picked up automatically. Keep those two headings as they are.
- **"Missing columns" message:** a column heading in the spreadsheet changed. Click **Remap Fields…**, fix the matches, click **Save Matches**, then **Generate** again.
- **Something on the slip is in the wrong place:** click **Edit Layout…**, drag it where you want it, then click **Save**. Sideways pages: pick **Landscape** next to the page size, or choose the ready-made **Landscape** layout. Big orders: set **Max items per bag** (e.g. 10).
- **"Something went wrong" message:** try again. If it keeps happening, send the file named in the message to the person who set up the app.
